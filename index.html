Where the cost comes from

Depth isn't additive. Each bar is the change between consecutive snapshots, credited to the pass that made it and grouped by what kind of change it was. Qiskit's stage names don't always match: SabreLayout runs in the layout stage but inserts SWAPs, so it counts as routing.

Same circuit, three chips

Each map shows the chip's connections and where every logical qubit starts. Thicker lines carry more two-qubit gates in the final circuit; dashed lines are connections where routing put a SWAP.

Estimated success probability

Product of (1 − error) over every gate, with the illustrative error rates below. These are assumptions for comparison, not device specifications, and they ignore idle errors, crosstalk and readout. Every target uses the same rates, so differences come from gate counts alone. Further reading on Wikipedia: Fidelity of quantum states, Quantum noise.
